The five descriptions, for your approval
Scan typeOne-line description
Quick
Looks at the 1000 most-used ports on every device and reports the known weaknesses it finds there. Takes about 2 to 5 minutes.hardware-agent/src/agent/tui/screens/scan_mode.py:15 · config.py:36 (vulners only)
Standard
Checks the 1000 most-used ports, then asks each device what it is running — file sharing, remote login, web pages — and matches that against known weaknesses. Takes about 5 to 10 minutes.scan_mode.py:16 · config.py:27 (smb, ssh, http scripts)
Extensive
Widens the search to 10000 ports and adds certificate and encryption checks. It also reports milder problems that the shorter scans leave out. Takes about 20 to 35 minutes.scan_mode.py:18 · config.py:38 (ssl-cert, ssl-enum-ciphers) · config.py:95 (min CVSS 3.0)
Max
Opens every port on every device and runs the full check list, and reports even the smallest findings. Takes 45 minutes or more.scan_mode.py:21 · port_discovery.py:40 (all ports) · config.py:97 (min CVSS 1.0)
NSPi
For sites with industrial equipment. It checks the usual ports plus the ones controllers and PLCs use, and adds the checks those machines need. Takes about 20 to 30 minutes.scan_mode.py:26 · config.py:52 (ports 502, 4840, 44818) · config.py:54 (modbus-discover, enip-info)

Note: the durations come straight from the scan-mode screen the box already ships (scan_mode.py), so they match what the hardware really does. The Mode column in the table above now uses these five real names — earlier rounds showed a made-up "Deep".
